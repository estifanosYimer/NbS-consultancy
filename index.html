Nature-based Solutions for water

Let nature
do the work.

Wetlands, forests and floodplains that hold, clean and store water. We help you put the right one in the right place, and prove that it works.

Spatial planning, design, coupled surface and groundwater modelling and remote-sensing monitoring for Nature-based Solutions, from site to catchment scale.

01 · Site selection

Find where nature
helps most.

We stack terrain, soil, water, land use and climate, weigh what matters to you, and map the best places for each solution.

AHP weighting and multi-criteria overlay of terrain, soils, hydrogeology, land cover and climate data, giving one suitability map per NbS type, with optional machine learning for aquifer recharge.

Suitability Explorer →
02 · Design

Shaped to
the place.

Size, depth, planting and layout, designed for the site and for the climate it will face.

Measure dimensioning and layout from site hydrology, soils and storage needs, with species and planting plans matched to local climate projections.

See the solutions →
03 · Modelling

Prove it
before you build.

Our models follow water above and below ground, so you see what changes with and without the measure: flood peaks, summer river flow and groundwater.

Distributed geohydrological modelling coupled to groundwater (SWAT+ with MODFLOW or gwflow), scenario runs with and without NbS, and climate stress tests.

Try with / without →
04 · Monitoring

Track it.
Learn. Repeat.

Satellites, drones and field sensors follow how the site performs, season after season.

Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2 and Landsat time series for vegetation, water and biomass, combined with drone surveys and in-situ sensors.

Meet nbsmonitor →
